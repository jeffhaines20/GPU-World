### Q-21 — The Competence Marriage

Implication: When every adult holds the same always-on clerical competence, the domestic trade in cognitive labour — "I do the money, you do the words" — stops being a true need, and with it goes the cover under which most long-married people asked each other for attention; only trades that ran on the body keep theirs.

Follows from: (1) A 2026-frontier model does the domestic clerical range — taxes, forms, letters — wherever the work, not the spouse's knowledge of the people, was the point. (2) Grant that asking was mostly a bid for attention. But long marriages dress bids as requests, because a dressed bid can be refused without a wound — "not now" refuses the task, not the person — and the dress needs a true need. Each partner now holds a share, present, effortless and better, so the need is false and both know it: the share takes not the wish but its dress, and a naked bid is a harder act. (3) Pre-2026 substitutes never tested this: software, search, accountants and early chatbots cost effort, money or a competence one partner lacked, so "easier to ask you" stayed true and the trade moved to whoever ran the tool; the share costs nothing to ask, while a spouse interrupts, indebts and may refuse. (4) The premise grants a GPU, not a body: cooking, lifting, driving, nursing stay true needs and keep their dress. (5) So the marriage whose pretexts were mostly cognitive — the professional couple's pride — loses most and the one that ran on the body least: a quiet inversion, roughly along class. Weakest: (2), that most people cannot make a naked bid; the card cannot say how many can.

For an ordinary person: Margaret, 58, married thirty years, wrote every letter the family sent while he did the bank, the car and the arguing. Now his share writes a better letter than she did and hers explains his pension better than he could. Nobody says "thanks for handling that," because nothing was handled. She tried once — "read this; I don't need you to" — and they both heard what the sentence was. What they say across the table is only what they want to say, and it is either not much or, she is startled to find, enough.

The strongest objection: Pretexts are infinite: "look at that bird" needs no competence gap, and a couple that loses the letter finds the weather by Friday; the bid was never about the letter. What the share mostly removes is the resented trades, and that is relief, not loss. Equality also ends "you couldn't manage without me," usually a lie. Marriages come out lighter, not emptier.

Either side: Is a bond that could only ask for attention under cover of need a lesser bond this world rightly exposes, or the only kind most people can sustain, which this world quietly removes?

Not the obvious version: The cliché is leaving for the AI. Nobody leaves; the share wants nothing and cannot be bid to — attention that costs nothing is worth nothing. It competes for the spouse's usefulness, not affection, wins without trying, and gives no cover back: two people in one room with nothing to do for each other and no way to ask.

Who it lands on: Margaret, who has worked out that her husband last asked her for anything not physical in 2031. She was the words of the marriage; the words are free now; and she does not know whether what is left is a marriage with nothing in it or with nothing in the way.

Status: revised

## What changed

Both reviewers named the same joint: the card conceded that asking was a bid for attention and never said why the bid would stop. Fixed by conceding fully and relocating the claim: the trade was never the glue but the cover, and a long marriage needs cover because a dressed bid can be refused without a wound; the share removes the cover, not the wish. Answered the pre-2026-substitutes objection with the cost gradient (old tools, early chatbots included, cost effort and an unevenly held competence, so the trade moved rather than vanished; the share reverses it). Narrowed "stops binding" to "stops being a true need"; softened the inversion to "roughly along class"; excluded trades where the spouse's knowledge of the people was the point. Could not fix: that most people cannot make a naked bid remains a generalisation from experience, now the named weakest step. Survives, narrower.
