# Premise cards

Every premise from every cell, in the fixed shape below. Struck cards stay on the page with the reason,
because the record of what was rejected is part of the evidence for what was chosen.

## The card shape

```
### P-NN — <cell> — <a title of convenience>
Conceit: <one sentence>
Who: <a person, and what they want>
Obstacle: <what stands in the way, and why it cannot simply be removed>
Turn: <the thing that changes and cannot change back>
Last image: <what the reader is left looking at>
Question: <the philosophical or psychological question the story leaves open>
Not the obvious version: <what the obvious version of this idea would be, and how this differs>
Closest published: <the nearest story the writer knows, and what differs>
Status: <generated | struck: reason | probed | finalist | chosen>
```
