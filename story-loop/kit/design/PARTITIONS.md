# The premise space, cut before anything is generated

Written before the first premise exists, so that six writers in six fresh contexts do not all land in
the same corner. Each cell goes to exactly one `premise-writer`, which sees its own cell and nothing
else.

**Axis 1 — the source of strangeness.** Where the story's science-fictional pressure comes from.

1. A technology and what it costs the people who use it.
2. A biology: another way of being alive, or ours altered.
3. A cosmology: the shape or age or indifference of the universe.
4. A social arrangement made possible, or unavoidable, by a change.
5. An alteration of physics or of time.
6. An alteration of mind: memory, identity, attention, what a self is.

**Axis 2 — the register**, from the brief. Every cell inherits it.

**The cells.** One per source of strangeness. The orchestrator writes the six here with any
narrowing the brief implies, and each writer receives one line: its cell, the register, the taste
brief, and the length target.
