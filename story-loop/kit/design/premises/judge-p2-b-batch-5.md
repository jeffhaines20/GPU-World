WOULD READ / WOULD NOT FINISH: P-31 at five thousand words, because the loss is carried entirely by objects and mouths — a comal, a word, a phone still recording — and the story has no villain to relieve it; P-50 I would not finish, because the card has already delivered its whole argument and the remaining pages are one woman deciding in one room, with the risk that the conference scene becomes the lecture the brief forbids.

MOST NOVEL QUESTION: P-28 — whether being *read* is itself a harm when nothing read is secret, and whether the right we always called privacy was in fact only a right to nobody bothering; it is novel because it dissolves the surveillance question the contest posed, relocating the panopticon from institutions to ordinary attention, and it proposes an answer (a polity agreeing not to look) that is simultaneously a moral achievement and forty people voting for the right to be hypocrites.
