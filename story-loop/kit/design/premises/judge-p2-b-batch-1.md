WOULD READ / WOULD NOT FINISH: P-40 at five thousand words, because the counter, the thirty-day window and the daughter typing her mother's number would only get better with room and the ending is already earned; P-52 I would not finish, because once the forecast is honest and nobody can be persuaded, the remaining pages have only one thing to demonstrate and the card has already demonstrated it.

MOST NOVEL QUESTION: P-29 — whether the right to be left alone includes the right to be left as you are, once free and infinite patience has made incapacity indefensible and turned capacity into a standing obligation; it is novel because the contest's own list treats the infinitely patient tutor as an unambiguous good and asks only whether it arrives, while this card asks what a society owes someone who declines to be improved, and locates the coercion not in the machine but in the sincere good faith of the state, the daughter, and the tutor alike.
