## The load-bearing claim

**The claim.** Friday at the bowl is Tess's first judgment of her own, and it is a spending, not a kindness. She finds Kit's bud by the silence in her ear, puts her own back and carries his up, and Rob says "Right." Everything in the ending is a cost of that act: Kit's reddening rim, Rob's look, the question she takes to Leeds. Read Friday as a sister being nice, and the ending becomes a sweet coda and the question is answered "rescue" by default.

**The steelman.** The revision builds Friday out of the week's own objects, and each part answers an earlier one:
- The bowl began with his hand at her ear, at the door. It ends with her hand at the bowl, her own ear as the test, and Kit's own fingers closing on the bud.
- The world's rule, "theirs alone", becomes the act. Hers speaks, so she puts it back. His is silent to her, so she carries it. She chooses the counsel she cannot hear.
- She acts without her bud, in front of the man who raised her without one. So the brief's question runs a second time, one generation down:
  - First time: Rob departed from the model, and Tess paid and audited him.
  - Second time: Tess departs, and Kit pays (the red rim, the three answers). He now holds a counsel that can audit her and that she cannot hear.
- That night she lies where Rob has lain since August: on the near side of a wall a child is whispering through, knowing, and not going in.
- On Saturday, Rob's look goes to Kit's ear, not to her at the door. That is the passage's last line played in daylight.

**The attack.** I attacked the event three ways, and it held each time.
- *As rescue, because Kit asked.* It holds. He asked for trains; he gets a red rim and three fathers. The week has shown the bud to be Tess's affliction as much as her counsel: the nights, the rim, three reasons and none of them hers. Handing it on is a contagion as much as a gift, and "in the whisper he learned from her" says so without a word.
- *As Rob's turn, not hers.* It holds. The passage has him stop mid-sentence and still say "Give it here." Friday gives him a reason to finish that change. The act is done openly, in a house whose language is silence, by an adult who has just shown with her own ear that she cannot hear what she is handing on.
- *As Thursday again.* It holds. Thursday was inaction and secret, and it used Kit. Friday is action and open, and it is done for him.

What does not hold is the spine's own account of the act. Its turn sentence names changes the passage made on Thursday (F2). Its price is paid in a currency Monday voided (F1). The beat is better than the sentences that describe it. A drafter builds to the sentences, so both findings rank first. Neither is MAJOR, because the right change and the right price are already there, in Beat 5's last sentence and in the ending image. The fix is to name them.

This sign-off covers this spine. A draft that plays "no run twelve" as the sacrifice, or stages Friday as the moment Rob learns, breaks it, and I will withdraw it.
