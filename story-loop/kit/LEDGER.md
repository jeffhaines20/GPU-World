# Ledger

One row per critic round. The largest gap is the single thing most worth fixing next; the approach is
how it is being fixed. The same largest gap surviving two rounds under the same approach means the
approach changes, in writing, before a third round, or the piece goes to the human.

The **Gate** cell is written only on the round a gate was signed at, and holds that gate (`G3`). It is
what "since the last gate" means anywhere else in the kit: the rounds after the last row carrying a gate.
