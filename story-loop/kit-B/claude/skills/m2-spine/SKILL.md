# M2 — The spine

1. Spawn `story-architect` with the chosen card, its probe, the taste brief, and the length target. It
   writes `story/SPINE.md` and `story/BIBLE.md`.
2. Spawn `depth-critic` and `adversarial-critic` on the spine and the probe, in fresh contexts. A MAJOR
   here is fixed here: the turn that is not a turn, the question that is a slogan, the ending that is
   the premise restated. Two rounds at most; a third means the premise is the problem, and that goes to
   the human with the runner-up premise beside it.
3. `close-review` on both reports.
4. Write the `INBOX.md` request for G2: the spine, the probe, and one question — "is this the story you
   want?" Fifteen minutes of the human's time at most, and their answer is a decision row.
5. Then the gate skill for G2.

**Where this fails.** A spine written as a summary of what happens rather than a structure of what
changes; skipping the critics because the spine is short; treating the human's "yes, fine" as a signed
gate.
