MOST NOVEL QUESTION: P-48 — what a polity should tax when its citizens' whole day is spent consulting a machine, and whether a state funded by need can honestly want its people to need less; it is novel because every other card here asks about judgment, liability or patience, whereas this one finds the fiscal question nobody in the contest's list thought to ask, and answers it with an institution that is good, popular, undodgeable, and quietly hoping nobody learns.
