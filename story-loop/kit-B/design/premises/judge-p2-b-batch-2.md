WOULD READ / WOULD NOT FINISH: P-25 at five thousand words, because the six-day clock, the offer at the ditch and the woman who is her own law's exception give the philosophical question a body and a deadline; P-48 I would not finish, because nothing in it can wound the person who signs, and a story whose only stake is a budget line will run out before its idea does.

MOST NOVEL QUESTION: P-41 — whether a community has a right to its load-bearing fictions, and whether a machine that answers everyone privately and identically is more corrosive to those fictions than a state that counts in public. It is novel because it locates the danger not in surveillance or exposure but in the manufacture of common knowledge with no author: nobody is watched, nobody is revealed, nobody decides, and a settlement that survived seventy years of suspicion cannot survive everyone knowing that everyone asked.
