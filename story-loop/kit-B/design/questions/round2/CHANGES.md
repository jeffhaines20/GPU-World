# What the revisers changed (round 2 reviewers do not see this)

## Q-05 — The Unasked Question

Reviewer A (asking may normalize into a lookup): the argument no longer needs asking to feel like accusation. Step (4) now argues normalization makes not-asking rarer and more marked, so more of an act; the dilemma sharpens rather than collapsing into duty.

Reviewer B (the model only reweighs what she tells it): step (2) narrowed. The card no longer claims the model infers better than she could, only that it holds what she has told it in ordinary use, forgets nothing, wants nothing, and answers in a second, removing the labour that let suspicions die. This leans on one reading of the premise: a 2026 assistant retains what its owner tells it. Without it the argument survives weaker (typing five facts takes a minute, not a week).

Conceded: the mole, with no one to trust; and the case where she already knows, where the premise adds only speed. Survives.

## Q-06 — Practicing on People

Reviewer A (step 2): dropped the assertion that deferring builds only deferring. The consult-decide-see loop is absorbed: watching counsel fail teaches where it fails, but what to do instead is learned only by doing otherwise; the model's constant presence does not break the loop, it makes every departure elective, so its costs become chosen rather than accidental. Reviewer B (step 4): dropped "worse expected outcome", which the premise does not guarantee on intimate cases and which contradicted step 3. The debt is now the apprentice's inability to know in advance whether her sense beats the counsel; the parent who knows owes nothing. Implication and the son's list resharpened to match: which cases did the father know would work? Objection strengthened to its best form, depart only after visible failure. Not fixed: whether departing builds judgment faster than the model's failures would; step 2 stays the contested joint. Survives, narrowed.

## Q-08 — The Promise Against the Forecast

Fixed: step (1) no longer rests on "a number once seen is not unseen"; the model can be asked "and if I promise?", so it prices the promise and its number contains the promise's predictive content by construction, at no more than friend-level wisdom. Step (2) now respects the privacy structure: her share holds her history of him, not his, and yields her own number. Step (3) locates the lost innocence: estimating was always possible, but saying so broke the frame; now it is private and free. Step (4) derives legibility from universality: she knows he asked or abstained without knowing which, and asking him is the crisis. Narrowed: to promises between intimates; strangers keep the old promise. Could not fix: how much of a life a 2026 model retains is not given by the premise. The implication survives, narrowed.

## Q-11 — The Ghost Estate

Both reviewers named the same weakest step, the leap from "context compounds" to "worth more than a house." I argued and narrowed it: the shadow is priced as goodwill already is under business as usual, a year or two of fees, plus a continuity premium, and no more; it is information, not capability, so the ceiling holds; it is not fungible, so it is an estate, not an exchange, which answers "every buyer has fourteen years of their own." "Most valuable thing a person can leave" is now confined to practice-owners; a wage worker's is a keepsake or the employer's. I conceded that "cannot run both" is not a hardware fact and relocated the displacement to whose corrections the day goes to. The objection now opposes the conclusion instead of deflating the asset. Not fixed: the continuity premium stays an assumption, now the named weakest step. Survives, narrowed.

## Q-12 — The Ward's Share

Leasing is gone. The card now argues from the premise alone: it fixes title to a share and is silent on direction, because for a competent adult the two coincide; guardianship, which business as usual supplies, separates them for wards. No engine is handed to anyone; its work product is sold, like anyone's. Naples and Lourdes now run on that mechanism. The trust objection is answered (a trust holds earnings, not direction). Tilt fixed: the objection carries the care side (before 2040 she was a cost; now her share pays her bed), and Lourdes's sum and wish agree, so the ending no longer settles it. Narrowed: the state's births-as-capital sum is dropped; the claim is household-scale. Not fixed: "de facto pooling" reads the premise's silence, not a grant; a reader who extends the ban to direction gets idle ward-engines, which the card argues no state chooses. Survives.

## Q-14 — The Rights Run

Step (4), conceded and narrowed: in strong cases the trial penalty and detention, not ignorance, hold the plea rate; the model moves only the fraction priced in information, and the chain argues that fraction suffices because the system has no slack, so a doubling breaks it, and motion volume rises even where pleas hold. The 20%-quadrupling arithmetic is gone. Step (6), answered two ways: each quiet lever pulled harder costs a contested hearing or is itself a written rule, and audibility no longer depends on the state confessing, since every citizen's model reads the public docket. Step (7) anchored in England's 2025 review. The crisis sharpened: a hidden-price plea; the defender becomes the one urging the deal. Not fixable: the size of the information-priced fraction is an empirical bet; if it is tiny and courts absorb a doubling, the run is a ripple. Survives, narrowed.

## Q-15 — No Cognition Without a Person

Fixed (reviewer A, step 1): the card no longer claims institutions have no cognition. The premise lets a state own ordinary compute; the card says so and rebuilds the asymmetry on what is granted: the share in the hand is the person's, cannot be taken (no one has none), and is the state's equal because the frontier froze. The implication is scoped to coercion that still needs a hand. Narrowed (reviewer B, step 4): the claim is no longer that soldiers ask but that the excuse of ignorance dies; a ban on asking is itself an answer, and "I did not ask" is willful blindness. The model's honesty is limited to what it is told and asked; the vignette drops the omniscient "what the villagers are doing," and the objection gains the drone. Not fixed: whether knowing changes conduct (5), still the named weakest step. The implication survives, narrower.

## Q-21 — The Competence Marriage

Both reviewers named the same joint: the card conceded that asking was a bid for attention and never said why the bid would stop. Fixed by conceding fully and relocating the claim: the trade was never the glue but the cover, and a long marriage needs cover because a dressed bid can be refused without a wound; the share removes the cover, not the wish. Answered the pre-2026-substitutes objection with the cost gradient (old tools, early chatbots included, cost effort and an unevenly held competence, so the trade moved rather than vanished; the share reverses it). Narrowed "stops binding" to "stops being a true need"; softened the inversion to "roughly along class"; excluded trades where the spouse's knowledge of the people was the point. Could not fix: that most people cannot make a naked bid remains a generalisation from experience, now the named weakest step. Survives, narrower.

## Q-25 — Solitude becomes a verb

Narrowed the implication to a split: isolation bundled being unanswered with being held, the share restores only the first, and the hole survives wherever a body is held, answering reviewer B's step (3): inalienable as allocation, not access. Reviewer A's weakest step is conceded: the husband keeps fear and money, two levers of three. Step (2) is narrowed to answer, not faces or touch; its residual doubt, a mind that cannot disagree from its own eyes, is the named weakest step, and the found-solitude half does not rest on it. Rebuilt "either side" around whether the loss is real, not whether solitary was worth keeping. Gave the novice a want, a price and a reason the share is in her cell; the nurse a second crisis. Unfixed: whether answer without perception keeps anyone sane. Survives, narrowed, with the trade honestly worse: half a reform for all of found solitude.

## Q-26 — The lucid remainder

Answered reviewer-a on (4): the premise's "always on" means incapacity cannot mute the share; the law can deny its answers standing, and the card now grants that it will (her record to the incapacity date, extrapolation after). That dates the problem rather than ending it, and a guardian's substituted-judgment test still asks what the share will say. Answered reviewer-b on (1): conceded that a 2026 model follows its prompter, so after incapacity the family holds the prompt; folded that into the objection, both sides, and the teacher's Tuesday, where she now rehearses her son's questions. Narrowed the implication: "cannot be switched off" covers the engine; the record can be burned, as a diary can, only knowingly. Named persistent memory as the assumption it is and moved the weakest step to (2); it cannot be derived from the granted text. The implication survives, narrower and still two-sided.

## Q-27 — Nothing said is evidence

Step (4) conceded to reviewer B: the signal's cost is not zero; its differential cost between faker and genuine is. Its two costs, material and words, are split: the share zeroes words for everyone (the fairness gain) and gives the faker the material (the loss); surviving effort sorts diligent from idle, not changed from unchanged. Step (5) conceded to reviewer A: the record is evidence of conversion, often better; the claim is narrowed to the interval between a change and its record, and to conduct an institution can see. Reviewer B's "waved at" answered: most decisions were on the file; the encounter was only what could overrule it. "Neither forbid nor discount" sharpened to "nor tell coached from uncoached." Objection strengthened with A's loss-is-a-gain. The man's change dated so the crisis is the narrowed claim. Not fixable from the premise: whether encounters ever overruled files is empirical. Survives, narrowed.
