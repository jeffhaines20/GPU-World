rigour: Step (2) is the crack — the card asserts both models "asked honestly" converge on "both readings defensible," but a 2026-quality model is far likelier to give confidently divergent verdicts shaped by each prompter's framing, and the card's own footnote waves this away rather than answering it.

Q-04 | importance=5 | rigour=4 | novelty=4 | arguability=4 | story=4
importance: It asks what a person's mind is for once every competence they have is duplicated at a fixed, known ceiling, which is the whole-life question the premise most directly forces.
rigour: The chain from fixed ceiling to publicly mapped boundary to self-respect hanging on an unanswerable question is tight and needs no more capability than granted; the only slack is whether fourteen years really produces a crisp public line rather than a contested fog.
novelty: The specific move — not obsolescence but *definition by subtraction*, a boundary identical for everyone and knowable only through the thing being bounded — is a genuinely fresh framing of a much-discussed topic.
arguability: The objection (there is no residue, only status anxiety; the Chinese Room was always bad) is strong enough that a reader could finish convinced the whole crisis is vanity.
story: The 58-year-old engineer hunting for one thing he knows that his share does not is a scene with a want and a price, though the crisis is more interior than situational.
weakest step: The leap in (5) from "the boundary is known" to "self-respect turns on whether the overrule-act is a faculty" — most people whose competence is duplicated would simply stop asking, and the card does not earn why they cannot.

Q-07 | importance=4 | rigour=2 | novelty=4 | arguability=4 | story=4
importance: A culture that loses the machinery of blame and forgiveness at population scale is a deep change to moral life, affecting every parent and every harmed child.
rigour: Weak — the card needs (1) strong convergence of everyone's answers, (3) law promoting "I asked and followed" to the whole of due care, and (4) a serious traceable correlated error, and each of these is asserted rather than derived; in particular the premise says nothing about models converging, and personalized context plus prompting variance argues against it.
